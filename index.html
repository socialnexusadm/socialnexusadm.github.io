About the Nexus

A modern community for technology professionals in and around Charlotte.

Why this community exists

Charlotte IT Social Nexus is designed to bring people together beyond job titles. The group creates a place for meaningful networking, practical knowledge sharing, professional growth, and collaboration across cloud, infrastructure, AI, cybersecurity, data, software, and operations.

What this site manages

  • Upcoming community events and registrations
  • Past event highlights and speaker notes
  • Embedded YouTube sessions and photo galleries
  • Community announcements and membership links
